Celestial Owls brings together a series of paintings inspired by the symbolism, mystery and quiet presence of owls.

Owls have long been associated with wisdom, guidance and seeing what others cannot. Their quiet presence, nocturnal lives and watchful nature give them a sense of mystery that has fascinated people across cultures and generations.

Each species in the collection is native to Scotland, or a seasonal visitor, creating a subtle connection to place and heritage. Through these paintings, I wanted to bring together the character of the birds themselves with the meanings and sense of wonder we have attached to them.

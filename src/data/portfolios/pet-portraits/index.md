Pet Portraits celebrates the bond we build with the animals who become part of our lives.

A portrait can hold onto more than appearance. It can preserve the expression you know instantly, the personality behind it, and the memories attached to years of companionship. These paintings are created to honour that connection and give it a lasting place in the home.

The Finder’s Frequency explores the idea of sensing what cannot immediately be seen.

I was drawn to the owl as a creature able to navigate darkness and locate what is hidden from view. The painting carries that sense of instinct, perception and quiet certainty, making it a piece about noticing more, trusting what you sense, and finding your way even when everything is not yet clear.

Autumn’s Golden Threshold captures a brief meeting between seasons, with autumn still blazing in the branches as winter gathers below.

Some of the most beautiful moments are temporary by their very nature. This painting is about noticing them while they are here, allowing ourselves to fully experience what is changing rather than rushing ahead to whatever comes next.

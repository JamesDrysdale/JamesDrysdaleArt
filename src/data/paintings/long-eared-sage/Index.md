Long Eared Sage explores the kind of clarity that comes from stillness, and the perspective we gain when we give ourselves space to think beyond the immediate moment.

Featuring a long-eared owl beneath a star-filled sky, the painting brings together watchfulness, wisdom and quiet contemplation.

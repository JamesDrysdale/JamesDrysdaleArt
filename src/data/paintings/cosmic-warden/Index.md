Cosmic Warden imagines the owl as a silent guardian beneath an immense night sky.

Set between dark forest and open cosmos, the painting plays with ideas of watchfulness, mystery and quiet strength. At its heart, it is about holding your place at the edge of the known, while looking out toward something vast and full of possibility.

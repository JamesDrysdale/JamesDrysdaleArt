The Dream of the Woodland is about the kind of place we carry in our imagination: lush, peaceful and full of life.

There are times when we long for somewhere that feels almost untouched by the demands of everyday life. This painting holds onto that dream of retreat and renewal, a place of colour, growth and quiet where there is room simply to be.
